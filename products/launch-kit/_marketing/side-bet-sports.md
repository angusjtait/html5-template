# $2 Side-Bet — Single Sports Bet (the how-to)

**Reality check:** this is negative expected-value entertainment, not strategy.
The real goal rides on Engine B. Stake only the $2 you've set aside, and treat a
loss as the expected outcome. If it ever stops being fun, skip it.

## The honest catch: minimum deposits
Most AU online bookmakers have **minimum deposits of ~$5–$10**, not $2. So a
literal $2 online bet usually isn't possible without depositing more. Two ways
to keep it a true $2 stake:

### Option 1 — In person at a TAB (cleanest $2 bet)
- Walk into any **TAB** outlet (or a pub/club with a TAB).
- Minimum bet is typically **$1**, paid in cash — no account, no minimum deposit.
- Place a **$2 "Win" single** on a selection priced around **$2.00** (even money).
  If it wins you get ~$4 → the $2 is doubled.

### Option 2 — Online (if you'd rather)
- You'll likely need to deposit the bookmaker minimum (~$5–10). If you do, only
  *bet* $2 of it and withdraw the rest, or accept the larger stake is on you —
  not from the challenge's $2.

## How to choose the bet (best return rate)
- Pick a **single** (not a multi — multis have far worse odds/margin).
- Target odds **~1.90–2.10** ("pick'em" lines). This is the closest to a fair
  coin-flip, where the bookmaker margin bites least → best return rate (~90%).
- Simplest markets: head-to-head on a match, or a 50/50 prop. Bet a team/outcome
  you actually have a view on — it doesn't change the odds, but it's more fun.
