# Engine B — Productized service (highest-probability path to fast cash)

The idea: sell ONE real deliverable for $20–40 to someone you already know or a
local online group. I (Claude) build the entire thing using the Launch Kit as
the starting point — you just make the offer and relay details. One "yes" hits
the goal on its own, and it's far more reliable than waiting for cold Gumroad
sales.

---

## The offer

**"I'll build you a clean, mobile-friendly one-page website for $30."**
- Delivered in 2–3 days
- For: a small business, a side hustle, a tradie, a freelancer, an event, a CV/portfolio
- They give me: their text, logo/photos, colours, links
- I deliver: a finished, deployed page (free Netlify/GitHub Pages) + the files

Alternative framings depending on who you know:
- "Need a portfolio site? $30, live this week."
- "Launching something? I'll build your waitlist page for $25."
- "I'll modernise your old one-pager for $30."

Price low enough to be an easy yes, high enough to clear the $20 goal in one sale.

---

## Where to find ONE buyer (online only)
1. **Warm contacts first** — anyone you know who runs a side hustle, freelances,
   is job-hunting (CV site), or has a small business. This is the fastest yes.
2. **Local Facebook groups** — "[Your town] small business", "[Your town] buy
   nothing/community" (check group rules for service posts).
3. **Local biz with no/ugly website** — a café, PT, cleaner, photographer.
   Offer the demo page first (see message C).

## Delivery workflow (my side)
1. You forward me their details (text, links, colours, any logo/photos).
2. I pick the best Launch Kit template and fully customise it for them.
3. I hand you the finished folder + a one-click Netlify deploy / GitHub Pages steps.
4. You collect $30 (PayPal/bank transfer/cash-equivalent) and deliver.

## Why this is the safest bet for the 2-week window
- Doesn't depend on cold-traffic luck.
- One sale = goal met (and Gumroad sales become pure bonus).
- The Launch Kit means the build is fast and already high quality.
