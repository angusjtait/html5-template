# Engine B — Cold/Local Route Playbook (no warm contacts)

Goal: land ONE local business that wants a one-page website, paid $30–60 direct
(PayPal/bank transfer/cash). One yes = the whole $10→$20 challenge is won.

The winning tactic for cold outreach: **lead with a finished sample.** Don't ask
"do you want a website?" — show them one of *their* business that already looks
great. We've built the `04-local-business` template exactly for this.

---

## The 4-step play

### 1. Pick targets (you, ~15 min)
Find local businesses with **no website or an ugly/outdated one**. Easiest source:
- Google Maps your suburb for a trade/service — "lawn mowing [suburb]",
  "mobile mechanic", "dog grooming", "cleaner", "personal trainer", "café".
- On each listing, look for: no website link, or a broken/dated one, but an
  active phone number. Those are your best targets.
- Facebook local business pages with no linked website are gold.
- Aim for a shortlist of **5–8**.

### 2. I build a tailored demo (me, ~30 min each)
Send me, for one target:
- Business name, what they do, suburb, phone
- 2–3 things they offer + any photos/colours/logo you can grab from their FB/Maps
I'll customise `04-local-business` into a real-looking demo for *their* business.

### 3. You deploy + send (you, ~5 min)
- I'll give you the files; drop them on **Netlify Drop** for an instant live link
  (e.g. `evergreen-demo.netlify.app`).
- Send the message below with the live link.

### 4. Close + deliver
- On a yes: collect $30–60 (PayPal/bank). I finalise (their real photos, copy,
  contact form wired up). You hand over the live site + files.
- Optional upsell: "+$15/yr and I'll set it on your own domain like
  yourbusiness.com.au."

## Outreach messages

### Facebook / email — lead with the demo
> Hi [Name]! I came across [Business] and put together a quick sample website to
> show what a clean, mobile-friendly page could look like for you: [demo link]
>
> If you'd like it finished and live (your photos, your details, ready to share
> with customers), I can do it this week for just $30 — no obligation at all.
> Either way, feel free to keep the sample. Cheers!

### Facebook local group — open offer
> 👋 I'm building clean, mobile-friendly one-page websites for local businesses
> this week — $30, live in 2–3 days. Great for trades, services, cafés, or side
> hustles. Comment or DM and I'll send you a free sample for YOUR business. 🙌

### Follow-up (if no reply after 2–3 days)
> Hey [Name], just following up on the sample site I made for [Business] —
> [demo link]. Happy to tweak anything or answer questions. No pressure either way!

## Realistic conversion
Cold outreach converts low — expect **~1 in 8–12** to say yes. So line up a
shortlist of 8+, send a tailored demo to the 2–3 most promising, and post in 1–2
local groups. That volume makes one $30 sale very achievable inside 2 weeks.
