# Ko-fi Shop listing — copy/paste

**Why Ko-fi:** free plan = 0% platform fee, paid straight to your PayPal/Stripe
per sale, **no $100 threshold** (unlike Gumroad). This is now our primary
storefront for the 2-week goal. Keep Gumroad live too — just for its marketplace
discovery; treat any Gumroad income as a later bonus.

## Setup (one-time, ~5 min)
1. Sign up at **ko-fi.com** → verify email.
2. **Settings → Payments** → connect **PayPal** (and/or Stripe). Region: Australia.
   - Ko-fi: "Get paid directly" = funds hit your PayPal immediately on each sale.
3. Turn on the **Shop** (Ko-fi dashboard → Shop → enable).
4. **Add a digital product** → upload the zipped `launch-kit` folder
   (exclude the `_marketing` folder — that's yours, not the buyer's).
